# v0.14.0 — Online play, a new launcher and HD pack downloads

The first release of Def Jam Remastered from this repository.

## For players

- **Online matches for up to four players**: One on One, Team Match, Free For All and the
  special matches. A lobby where everyone ticks *Ready*, a fighter select where each player
  picks any fighter or the created fighter of one of their own profiles, then straight into
  the match. After it, everyone votes Rematch or Quit. The games exchange inputs every fight
  step and compare the fight state every second ([Online play](../netplay.md)).
- **No port forwarding for most hosts**: hosting asks the router to open the port (UPnP) and
  shows the address to give the others, with a Copy button; the port is closed again when
  the session ends.
- **A console-style launcher**: Continue (straight into your Story profile), Battle, Online,
  Main Menu, Profiles and Mods tiles over full-window art from your own data folder, with
  your profile's Story progress, cash and fighter. The settings pages share its look
  ([Launcher and overlay](../launcher-and-overlay.md)).
- **HD Fighters and HD Widescreen Menus download and update from the launcher**: Mods >
  Refresh catalogue. The catalogue is signed and hosted on this repository's `mods` release
  ([Mod management](../mod-manager.md)).
- **File mods**: packs can replace the game's own files (music, speech, models, arenas).
- **PS2 profiles carry over**: Story progress, cash, unlocks and the created fighter, from a
  PCSX2 memory card or an Action Replay MAX save ([Importing PS2 saves](../importing-ps2-saves.md)).
- **Fixed**: a white window that could stay up at start-up instead of the title (the timer
  thread was starved on one core).

## Under the hood

- Lockstep mode: a match plays the same at any frame rate from one seed; a per-step fight
  fingerprint catches a desync.
- The native test fixtures build and pass again with MSVC on the path.
- `scripts/publish-mods.py` publishes a pack version and signs a new catalogue revision.

Toolkit: `b578c7afbd008be1c3376ef5a4f89058ab42be86` (ScikuVRC/xboxrecomp). Setup compiles
the player's verified supported USA Xbox dump locally. No game executable or game assets are
distributed with this release; the HD packs on the `mods` release are derived artwork, whose
rights remain with their owners.

## Tested

- Windows 11: unit tests (with the native fixtures), release build, the game-free Windows
  Setup build and its asset check; online matches between two games on one PC; the launcher's tiles started into Story, Battle and the main menu; the
  router opened and closed the port on a real home router.
- Not run for this release: the full `regress.py`, macOS and Linux play, a real Setup install
  and Clean Update, and a friend joining from outside the host's network. See
  [Known issues](../known-issues.md#v0140).
